🎨 Draw-O-Matic 4000 👾
Draw-O-Matic 4000 is an interactive, arcade-style drawing prompt generator designed to spark wild creativity for quick drawing contests. It mixes and matches visual styles, pop culture icons, internet memes, and pure chaotic scenarios to create thousands of unique, unpredictable, and hilarious drawing prompts.
This app is tailored to balance creepy, cute, and cartoony aesthetics—bringing together everything from Roblox, Minecraft, and Marvel to baby Pibbles (pit bulls), the legendary derpy fish Sacabambaspis, and Ralph Lazar's chaotic Total Mayhem book universe.

✨ Features
￼ Tag-Based Dashboard: Customize your prompts by turning specific category pools on or off. Mix "Spooky & Gross" with "Foodie" or "Cute & UwU" with "Total Mayhem"!
￼ Zero-Repeat Session Memory: The machine remembers what it has picked during your session. You won't get a duplicate word until you've cycled through the entire active pool.
￼ Classic "Lost Gems" Restored: Includes legendary drawing favorites like Guest 666, Bacon Hair, sus crewmates, and radioactive toilets.
￼ Arcade Dynamics: Features retro grid animations, twinkling background stars, a rapid slot-machine rolling sequence, and interactive neon-glowing typography.
￼ Victory Confetti: Celebrates every finalized prompt with an explosion of colorful, gravity-bound physics particles.
￼ Fully Mobile Responsive: Tailored touch mechanics ensure a perfect experience on iPads, tablets, and mobile screens without accidental double-taps or zooming issues.
